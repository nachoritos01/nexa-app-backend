# Acceptable Use Policy

**Last updated:** [Date]

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") governs the use of the [Platform Name] platform operated by [Company Name]. By using the platform, you agree to comply with this policy.

## 1. Intended Use

The platform is designed for legitimate business operations including:
- Managing business workflows and processes
- Storing and organizing business data
- Collaborating with team members
- Serving customers through integrated portals

## 2. Prohibited Uses

You may **not** use the platform to:

### Illegal Activity
- Conduct or facilitate any activity that violates applicable laws or regulations
- Store, transmit, or distribute content that is illegal in your jurisdiction
- Facilitate fraud, money laundering, or other financial crimes

### Security Violations
- Attempt to access data belonging to other accounts or tenants
- Perform vulnerability scanning or penetration testing without prior written authorization
- Exploit any bug or vulnerability in the platform rather than reporting it responsibly
- Circumvent or disable any security feature or access control

### Abuse and Overuse
- Send spam, phishing, or unsolicited bulk messages through the platform
- Generate excessive automated requests that degrade service for other users
- Create multiple trial accounts to avoid subscription fees
- Use bots or scripts to scrape data from the platform

### Harmful Content
- Store or distribute malware, viruses, or any malicious code
- Host content that is defamatory, harassing, or threatening
- Store content that infringes on intellectual property rights of third parties

## 3. Content Guidelines

You are responsible for all content you upload or create on the platform. Content must:
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations
- Not infringe on the intellectual property rights of others
- Not contain personally identifiable information of individuals without proper consent
- Be relevant to legitimate business use

## 4. API Usage

If your plan includes API access:
- You must use API keys securely and not share them publicly
- Rate limits must be respected; excessive calls may be throttled
- API access must not be used to build a competing product
- Automated integrations should implement proper error handling and backoff

## 5. Resource Limits

- Each plan has defined resource limits (users, storage, API calls, etc.)
- Approaching a limit triggers a notification at **80% usage**
- Exceeding a limit provides a **7-day grace period** to upgrade
- After the grace period, the limited resource is restricted until you upgrade

## 6. Enforcement

Violations of this AUP may result in:
1. **Warning:** A written notice describing the violation and required corrective action
2. **Temporary suspension:** Service access is restricted until the issue is resolved
3. **Permanent termination:** Account is terminated without refund for severe or repeated violations

[Company Name] reserves the right to determine, at its sole discretion, whether a violation has occurred and to take appropriate action without prior notice in urgent cases.

## 7. Reporting Violations

If you become aware of any violation of this policy, please report it to abuse@[yourdomain].com.

## 8. Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Significant changes will be communicated at least **15 days** in advance via email.
